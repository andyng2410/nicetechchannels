## Main claim from the tweet

OpenAI says building apps is now easier with `Sites` in Codex. Codex can turn work, ideas, and plans into an interactive website or app that a team can explore, use, and share with a URL. The rollout starts with Business and Enterprise plans before expanding more broadly.

## Official article facts

- The article title is `Codex for every role, tool, and workflow`.
- OpenAI says Codex has more than 5 million weekly users.
- Non-developers make up about 20% of overall Codex users and are growing more than 3x as fast as developers.
- The broader update includes role-specific plugins, Sites, and annotations.
- Sites are in preview for Business and Enterprise customers.
- Sites can create interactive hosted websites and apps from ideas, analysis, and plans.
- Examples include dashboards, planners, review workspaces, project boards, galleries, lightweight tools, customer review pages, scenario planners, and launch hubs.
- Sites can be shared with anyone in the same workspace via URL.
- OpenAI mentions early partners including Vercel, Wix, Base44, Replit, Lovable, Figma, Webflow, and Emergent.
- Enterprise admins can enable Sites in admin settings.

## Community response

- Positive: many replies frame this as a big shift from app building to one-conversation prototyping.
- Competitive angle: several replies mention Lovable, Bolt, Replit, Webflow, and similar app builders.
- Practical concerns: users ask where apps are hosted, how state management and auth work, whether design quality is better, and when Pro/non-business users get access.
- Skeptical angle: some replies warn that a prototype is not the same as a reliable production tool, especially around security, scalability, messy workflows, and frontend quality.
